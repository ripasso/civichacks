## Why it is written this way

Built from the organizers' "Tips for your demo video" and the 20-point rubric:

- **Problem in the first 20 seconds**: names the challenge, who is affected (residents), what goes wrong (the record is scattered; people hear too late).
- **Solution working by 0:37**, well inside their 90-second mark.
- **One task, one resident, start to finish**: Sam (an example resident, labelled as such) follows the real 245 Eglinton Ave E rezoning, not a feature tour.
- **Shows what changes**: the real 30-page staff report and agenda page today, then two texts.
- **Names the data**: City of Toronto voting record, address points, agenda pages; no AI writes replies.
- **Upfront about what is real**: "Working today" vs "Not built yet" (rubric 5-point line: distinguishes working features from plans). The replay is explained and labelled.
- **Path forward**: what a city needs to pilot it, cost, and how other cities adapt it.
- **Every fact is checked against the records**: 59-storey towers and 30 pages (staff report page 1), votes 2-4, 6-0, 25-1, 12-11 (voting record), 0 wrong of 2,000 addresses (tests).

## Recording tips (from the organizers)

Quiet room, best microphone, record a 10-second test first. Read in your own natural voice. Save as .m4a, .mp3 or .wav and send it over; it gets laid onto the video.
