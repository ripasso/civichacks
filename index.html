What the replies are built from

Only official records
City of Toronto open data (council voting record, address points) and the city's own agenda item pages. No AI writes or summarizes the replies.
No guessing
An address near a ward boundary gets asked for its ward number. A comment deadline is only shown when the official page confirms it.
Your privacy
Addresses are used once to find the ward and not kept. WATCH keeps only your phone number and ward.
